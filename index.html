How it works

From sign-up to your first lead in a few minutes.

1

Register your company

Add your logo, brand color, and the products or services you offer. Optionally add a company video and profile document.

2

Create an event

Add the event name, venue and dates. You get a short link to a contact form branded with your company details.

3

Collect leads

Visitors fill in the form on their phone. Each submission appears in your dashboard right away, filtered by event.

For vendors

Stop collecting business cards and typing up sign-up sheets. Every contact is captured once, correctly, and kept in one place.

  • A contact form with your logo, brand color and services
  • Showcase your company video and profile document
  • Leads from every event in one dashboard, updated live
  • A separate link for each event you attend

For attendees

Visitors open the link on their own phone and are done in under a minute.

  • No app to install and no account to create
  • Just a name, email and phone number, plus an optional comment
  • A confirmation email with the vendor's contact details
  • Consent to follow-up is asked for up front
