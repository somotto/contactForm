Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Event Connector?

The Event Connector is a dedicated lead capture platform created specifically for event vendors, exhibitors, and networking professionals.

Why does this platform exist?

It exists to solve the challenge of lost connections at events by providing a centralized, efficient way for vendors to capture, organize, and manage business leads directly from trade shows and networking events.

Who is this website designed for?

It is built for vendors, exhibitors, event marketers, and sales representatives who participate in physical or virtual events and need to streamline their attendee engagement and lead tracking.

What features does the platform offer?

Through the Vendor Dashboard, users gain secure portal access to ensure event leads are captured accurately and securely.

How do I get started with using the platform?

Existing vendors can sign in using their registered credentials, while new vendors can set up an account by selecting the Register here option on the main dashboard page.
