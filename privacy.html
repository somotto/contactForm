Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use or interact with TheEventConnector.com, we collect personal data provided directly during event registrations, including:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Direct Telephone Number
  • Additional form comments or notes submitted by users

2. How We Use Information

Information collected through TheEventConnector.com is used solely to:

  • Facilitate event registration and lead capture services for organizers and vendors.
  • Deliver notifications, event updates, and relevant business communications.
  • Maintain system functionality, audit tracking, and operational security.

3. Data Handling & Security

  • Confidentiality: Submissions processed via our lead capture platform are treated as confidential internal data.
  • Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or rent user personal data to third-party data brokers. Data is shared exclusively with the specific event host or business vendor associated with your registration form.
  • Security: We employ technical controls to safeguard registration data against unauthorized access.

4. Consent & User Rights

By submitting an event registration form on our platform, you consent to being contacted by email or telephone regarding your registration. You may withdraw consent or request access to and deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting the platform support team or the hosting vendor directly.
