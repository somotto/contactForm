1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to TheEventConnector.com (referred to herein as "TheEventConnector," "the Platform," "we," "us," or "our"). TheEventConnector.com is an event vendor registration and discovery platform designed to connect event organizers with qualified vendors, service providers, and industry professionals across a wide range of event types and categories. Our Platform enables vendors to register and showcase their services, and enables event organizers to discover, evaluate, and engage with vendors suited to their event needs.

We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of every individual who interacts with our Platform. This Privacy Policy explains how TheEventConnector.com collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information when you access or use our website, services, and related digital properties. We take our responsibilities under applicable data protection laws seriously and are dedicated to ensuring that your personal information is handled with care, transparency, and respect.

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of TheEventConnector.com, including event organizers, registered vendors, business partners, and general visitors to the Platform, regardless of location. By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Policy, please do not use the Platform.

This Privacy Policy should be read in conjunction with our Terms of Service, which govern your use of the Platform more broadly. Capitalized terms not defined herein shall have the meanings ascribed to them in our Terms of Service.

  • Do Not Track: Some browsers transmit "Do Not Track" signals. The Platform currently acknowledges these signals on a best-efforts basis; however, no universal standard for responding to such signals has been established.

6. Data Storage and Security

6.1 Where Your Data Is Stored

Your personal information is stored on servers located in the United States. As a result of our use of third-party service providers and cloud infrastructure, your data may also be processed or accessed in other jurisdictions. Where international data transfers occur, we take steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the use of standard contractual clauses or other approved transfer mechanisms.

6.2 Security Measures

We implement a comprehensive set of technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our Platform is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL) protocols
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive personal data stored on our servers is encrypted using AES-256 or equivalent encryption standards
  • Access Controls: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis, with role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, and audit logging
  • Regular Security Audits: We conduct periodic security assessments, vulnerability scans, and penetration tests to identify and remediate potential weaknesses in our systems
  • Employee Training: Our staff receive regular training on data protection obligations, security best practices, and incident response procedures
  • Vendor Security Requirements: Third-party service providers are required to maintain security standards commensurate with the sensitivity of the data they process on our behalf

While we strive to protect your personal information using these measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage users to take steps to protect their own accounts, including using strong, unique passwords and enabling two-factor authentication where available.

6.3 Data Retention Periods

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. The following retention periods apply:

Depending on your jurisdiction of residence, you may have certain rights with respect to your personal information. TheEventConnector.com is committed to honoring those rights and facilitating their exercise in a timely and transparent manner.

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information that TheEventConnector.com holds about you, along with information about how we use it, with whom we share it, how long we retain it, and from where we collected it.

Where processing is based on your consent or on the performance of a contract with you, you have the right to receive a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and to request that we transmit that information to another service provider where technically feasible.

If you believe that TheEventConnector.com has not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority in your jurisdiction. 8. International Privacy Rights & Global Compliance

TheEventConnector.com is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy rights of all users, regardless of where they are located. This Section describes our approach to global privacy compliance and the rights available to you under applicable international, regional, and national privacy laws.

8.1 Scope of Global Coverage

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with major international privacy regulations, including but not limited to:

  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — Regulation (EU) 2016/679
  • The UK GDPR — as retained in UK law under the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018
  • Kenya's Data Protection Act (DPA) — November 2019
  • Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
  • Australia's Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)
  • Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) — Law No. 13,709/2018
  • Other applicable regional and national privacy laws as may be in effect in the jurisdictions where our users reside

8.2 Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & Similar Frameworks)

Where required by applicable law, we process your personal data on one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity — Processing is necessary to provide and fulfill the services you have requested, including account creation, vendor registration, and event management features.
  • Legitimate Interests — Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, such as improving our Platform, preventing fraud, maintaining security, and communicating service-related updates, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Consent — Where you have provided explicit, freely given, and informed consent, such as for marketing communications or the placement of non-essential cookies. You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.
  • Legal Obligation — Processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation to which TheEventConnector.com is subject, including tax, accounting, and regulatory requirements.

8.3 Your Rights Under Applicable Law

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights with respect to your personal data. We are committed to honoring these rights in accordance with applicable law:

  • Right to Be Informed — The right to receive clear and transparent information about how your personal data is collected, used, and shared.
  • Right of Access — The right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification — The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure — The right to request deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten"), subject to applicable legal exceptions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing — The right to request that we limit the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability — The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Right to Object — The right to object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or carried out for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — The right to withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right Not to Be Subject to Automated Decision-Making — The right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint — The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority or supervisory body if you believe your rights have been infringed.

8.4 International Data Transfers

TheEventConnector.com is based in the United States. Your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in countries outside your home country, including the United States, which may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

When we transfer personal data internationally, we implement appropriate safeguards to ensure that your data receives an adequate level of protection, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) — as approved by the European Commission or applicable supervisory authority
  • Adequacy Decisions — where the destination country has been recognized as providing an adequate level of data protection
  • Other Legally Recognized Mechanisms — such as binding corporate rules or derogations permitted under applicable law
